The pattern

01 / measurements taken

Every neighborhood is measured differently — its streets, its risk, its history. My work starts by taking those measurements seriously: walking the block, reading the parcel data, sitting with the people who'll actually wear the outcome.

From there it's pattern-making — buffers, indicators, equity overlays — cut to fit the specific shape of a specific place, rather than draped over it like a generic template.
