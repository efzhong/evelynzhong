About

Background

B.S. in Urban Studies and Planning, and an M.S. in Emerging Transportation Systems from University of Southern California. My professional background spans local development review at Caltrans District 7 and integrated planning and programming at SCAG.

My research and project work sits at the intersection of environmental impact, land use, and transportation studies. You'll find me currently assisting with GIS on the Healthy Ageing and Urban Spaces (HAUS) study, led by Dr. Ester Cerin at Australian Catholic University. I code primarily in Python, with growing SQL and JavaScript skills.
